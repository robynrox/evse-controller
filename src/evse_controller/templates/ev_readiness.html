EV Readiness

Set a one-off state-of-charge target and the time it is needed, or use the weekly schedule for a regular departure. Before the target time the planner works out the cheapest path to reach it. From the target time the target becomes a floor: the car may keep charging, or discharge, but not below the target until the trip is complete, the plug is removed, or a scheduled hold ends.
