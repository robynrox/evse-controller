EV Readiness

Set a one-off state-of-charge target and the time it is needed. Before that time the planner works out the cheapest path to reach it. After that time the target becomes a floor: the car may keep charging, or discharge, but not below the target until you mark the trip complete or remove the plug. If the target cannot be reached, the controller charges as much as it can and reports the shortfall.
