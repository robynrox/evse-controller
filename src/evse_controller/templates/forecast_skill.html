How to read this

Forecast vs actual is the total error we experience. Model with actual weather reruns the same model with observed temperature/GTI; the difference between the forecast and that line is the weather-input error. The difference between that line and measured power is the model error. A total error dominated by the weather component points at the forecast; a total error dominated by the model component points at the model or the measurement.

Solar has no P90 band yet; its model is a point forecast plus the historic distribution. Heat-pump P90 comes from the fitted temperature/slot model. The historic heatmap is the per-slot distribution over the lookback window, not a single persistence line.
